Aroha

A private assistant run by Te Kawa Consultancy Limited, Aotearoa New Zealand.

Aroha is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

With the owner's permission, Aroha reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to give a morning briefing, and can send email, tidy the inbox and add calendar events when the owner asks and approves.
